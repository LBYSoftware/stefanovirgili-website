01 / Support leadership

Support is where trust is won or lost.

I lead technical support at ReadyOn AI as Founding Global Head of Technical Support, building the team, the processes and the tooling from the ground up.

Before that I worked through the hard end of support at fast-growing SaaS and fintech companies, including Zip, Plaid and Smartsheet, where I handled Tier 2 escalations. That's where I learned how good support turns frustrated customers into loyal ones.
